Know the symptoms

Signs it's time to call for appliance repair

Small faults get expensive when they're ignored. If any of these sound familiar, get it looked at promptly.

Your dryer isn't heating

Clothes come out damp after a full cycle, or the drum runs cold from start to finish.

Hard tumbling or loud banging

A dryer or washer that thuds and shakes is telling you something inside has worn or come loose.

A burning smell

Any scorched or burning odour from an appliance should be checked immediately — stop using it and call.

The fridge isn't cooling

Food spoiling early, or a refrigerator that simply never gets cold enough to be safe.

Constant clicking

A refrigerator that clicks on and off repeatedly is usually a starting or compressor fault.

Freezer works, fridge doesn't

A classic airflow or defrost problem — and one of the most common calls we take.

Water on the floor

A leaking washer or dishwasher spreads water damage fast. Shut it off and get it diagnosed.

Cycles that never finish

A dishwasher or washer that stalls mid-cycle, or won't drain at the end of one.
